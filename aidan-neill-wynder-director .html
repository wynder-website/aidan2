He took up the role in September 2026, bringing with him a rare combination: five years of senior-level digital marketing experience with some of the largest conglomerates in London and the Far East, and first-hand proof that he can build a brand people trust at scale.

Aidan is recognised as an expert in his field and a pioneer in the digital marketing space. He understands, better than most, how today's consumer discovers, researches and commits to a high-value purchase. At Wynder, he is applying that knowledge to one of the most enduring categories in luxury retail.

The Cazoo blueprint, applied to luxury watches

Wynder's vision draws directly on one of the most striking stories in recent British retail. Cazoo, founded in 2018 by Alex Chesterman, the entrepreneur behind Zoopla and LoveFilm, set out to change how the UK bought used cars. Its model was bold and simple: buy every car outright, recondition it to a consistent standard, sell it entirely online, and deliver it to the customer's door.

The impact was dramatic. Cazoo launched in 2019 and, within around two years, had become one of the most recognisable consumer brands in the country. In 2021 it listed on the New York Stock Exchange at a valuation of roughly $7 billion. Above all, Cazoo proved something the industry had doubted: British consumers will buy high-value items online, without seeing them in person, when they trust the business selling them.

Wynder takes that blueprint and applies it to a category arguably even better suited to it. Luxury watches are compact, high in value, easy to store and simple to ship securely. They hold their value well and command a passionate, global buyer base.

Why the market needs Wynder

The pre-owned luxury watch market has grown rapidly, yet much of it remains fragmented. Buyers navigate private sellers, inconsistent pricing and real uncertainty over authenticity and condition. Sellers face slow, opaque processes and lowball offers.

Wynder is designed to fix both sides. Rather than acting as a marketplace that simply connects strangers, Wynder buys watches outright and owns its inventory. One accountable business stands behind every timepiece, from the moment it is acquired to the moment it reaches the customer.

"With most marketplaces, you're trusting a stranger. With Wynder, you're buying from us. We own every watch we sell, we've checked every watch we sell, and we stand behind every watch we sell."

Aidan Neill

How Wynder operates

Six principles shape every part of the Wynder experience, for buyers and sellers alike.

We own our stock

Wynder buys every watch outright, so customers deal directly with the business, not a third-party seller.

Authentication first

Every watch is inspected and verified before it is listed, giving buyers complete confidence in what they are getting.

Fully online, end to end

Customers browse, buy and sell from their phone or laptop, through a process built for how people shop today.

Transparent pricing

Clear prices with no haggling, hidden fees or guesswork.

A fair way to sell

Owners can sell their luxury watch directly to Wynder through a straightforward, professional process.

Built for the UK and Europe

Wynder serves collectors and first-time buyers across the UK and European markets.
