About Aidan Neill

Aidan Neill is a UK digital marketing expert, a former TikTok creator and the Director of Wynder.

Known for his instinct for how modern audiences think and buy, Aidan has spent his career at the point where brands meet people online. He has worked with some of the largest conglomerates in London and the Far East, built a social media following of more than 500,000 people from scratch, and in September 2026 took up the role of Director at Wynder, the UK business bringing authenticated pre-owned luxury watches online.

Aidan is widely regarded as an expert in his field and a pioneer in digital marketing. His work rests on one belief: trust is the most valuable thing a brand can build, and it is built in public.

Aidan Neill, digital marketing expert

Aidan has five years of experience in digital marketing at the highest level. He has worked with some of the largest conglomerates operating across London and the Far East, helping major organisations reach, engage and convert customers in fast-moving, competitive markets.

Working across two of the world's leading commercial centres gave Aidan a genuinely international view of marketing. He understands how audiences behave across cultures and platforms, how high-value purchase decisions are made online, and how the strongest brands earn attention rather than buy it.

Aidan Neill on TikTok

Aidan proved his expertise by doing it himself. His TikTok channel grew to more than 500,000 followers, making him an influential creator on one of the world's fastest-growing platforms.

His audience carried real commercial value: at his peak, brands paid Aidan £850 per sponsored post to reach his followers. He has since stepped back from TikTok to focus on building businesses, bringing everything he learned about audiences, content and credibility with him.

"Building an audience showed me that trust is earned in public, every single day. People don't just buy a product anymore. They buy into the people and the standards behind it. That's exactly what we're building at Wynder."

Aidan Neill

Aidan Neill, Director of Wynder

As Director of Wynder, Aidan leads the company's strategy, brand and digital growth. Wynder buys, authenticates and sells pre-owned luxury watches online, serving buyers and sellers across the UK and Europe.

Unlike a marketplace that connects strangers, Wynder owns every watch it sells. Aidan's role is to make that promise visible: to build a brand that a new generation of buyers trusts with one of the most meaningful purchases they will make.

  • We own our stockWynder buys every watch outright, so customers deal directly with the business.

  • Fully online, end to endBrowse, buy and sell from a phone or laptop.
  • Transparent pricingClear prices with no haggling, hidden fees or guesswork.
  • A fair way to sellOwners can sell their luxury watch directly to Wynder.
  • Built for the UK and EuropeServing collectors and first-time buyers alike.
